Install on iPhone / iPad

1
Open this page in Safari

Use Safari for the clearest Home Screen installation flow.

2
Tap Share

Tap the Share button in Safari.

3
Choose “Add to Home Screen”

Confirm Add. Then launch NILTASK from its Home Screen icon.

4
Enable notifications after login

Open the installed app, sign in, and allow notifications when NILTASK asks.

Install manually

1
Open the browser menu

Open your browser menu.

2
Choose Install App

Depending on the browser it may say “Install NILTASK”, “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

3
Confirm installation

NILTASK will then open in its own app window and update automatically from the website.

Push notifications: supported through NILTASK Web Push. After installation and login, allow notification permission. The same server-to-Service-Worker route is used across supported PWA platforms.
